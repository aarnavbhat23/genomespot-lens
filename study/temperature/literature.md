# Literature comparison and novelty boundary

Searched primary publications and paper-linked resources on 20 October 2026
using combinations of encrypted/cryptic AMP, ecology, temperature, phylogeny,
composition, homologous proteins and GenomeSPOT. This is a scoped search, not a
systematic review or proof of priority. “Not demonstrated” below describes the
checked paper's reported analyses, not a claim that no supplement/code can exist.

| Closest work | Already established | Difference targeted here |
|---|---|---|
| [Archaeome / archaeasins, 2025](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41564-025-02061-0) | Published APEX catalog, genus variation and selected experimental validation | Reconstruct source exposure and test physiology using verified protein subsets, independent temperature and ancestry controls |
| [Lactobacillaceae encrypted AMPs, 2025](https://doi.org/10.1186/s40168-025-02145-3) | Genomic/taxonomic and habitat-associated AMP distributions; candidate activity measurements | A generic ecology→AMP claim is already occupied; focus on quantitative growth temperature and sampling/composition controls |
| [EEMC, 2026](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-026-71145-0) | Large extremophile genome resource and AMP discovery using protein language models | Do not claim extremophile AMP discovery as new; target a controlled reanalysis of encrypted fragments within homologous source proteins |
| [Arctic hydrothermal microbiomes, 2026](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12866-026-05098-1) | Metagenomic/transcriptomic AMP discovery, taxonomic profiles, predictions and experimental tests | Discovery in hot environments alone is not novel, and predicted activity needs validation |
| [Thermophilic amino-acid bias with phylogenetic controls, 2010](https://doi.org/10.1186/1471-2148-10-263) | Temperature/composition relations can remain after accounting for shared ancestry | Temperature-associated amino-acid changes may explain AMP predictor output; composition adjustment does not distinguish a biological mediator from prediction bias |
| [HECT encrypted AMP signature, 2016](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5220581/) | Evolutionary analyses of encrypted regions within a eukaryotic protein family | Within-family cryptic peptide evolution is not itself new; proposed scope is comparative archaeal physiology over many verified families |
| [GenomeSPOT](https://github.com/cultivarium/GenomeSPOT) | Physiology predictions from genome/proteome features with phylogenetic evaluation | Reuse the tool and preserve flags; test dependence on its shared sequence input, rather than claiming a new physiology predictor |

A defensible candidate contribution is a reproducible **comparative test** asking
whether temperature-associated encrypted-AMP density survives source mapping,
uneven annotation coverage, composition and ancestry controls, plus agreement
with within-homology contrasts. Neither statistical significance nor a null
result automatically makes a paper. A convincing manuscript needs adequate
coverage/power, sensitivity/replication and independent measured activity for
claims about actual antimicrobial function. No “highest novelty” or “first ever”
claim is supported by this search.
